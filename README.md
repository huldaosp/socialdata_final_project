# Danish Food Safety — Final Project

Final project for **Social Data Analysis and Visualization (02806)** at the Technical University of Denmark.

## About

This project analyses the **Danish Smiley scheme** — Fødevarestyrelsen's (Danish Veterinary and Food Administration) public database of food safety inspection results. Every food business in Denmark receives an inspection score from 1 (no remarks) to 4 (serious issues), and results are published openly.

We explore:
- Where food safety risk concentrates by business type and cuisine
- How risk is distributed geographically across Denmark's municipalities
- Whether failed inspections lead to measurable improvement in subsequent inspections

## Deliverables

- **Website** — a story-driven, interactive site presenting the findings for a non-technical audience
- **Explainer Notebook** — [`final_project.ipynb`](final_project.ipynb) contains the full data analysis, methodology, and visualizations behind the website
