Geography of bad inspections

Where do bad inspections happen?

The map shows the ratio of failed inspections in each kommune relative to the national average. Areas shown in red have a higher share of failed inspections than average, while green areas have fewer. Use the dropdown to filter by business category.

↗
Geographic variation exists
The greater Copenhagen area stands out — Frederiksberg (2.36×), Vallensbæk (2.40×), and København (1.89×) are all elevated. Some Jutland kommuner like Odder (2.26×) also stand out, though for less obvious reasons.
△
Use the filter to explore
The pattern shifts when you filter by business type, suggesting that geography and business type composition are linked. But as the previous sections show, what kind of business you are predicts inspection outcomes far more reliably than where in Denmark you are located.
ⓘ
Small samples are noisy
Vallensbæk has only ~300 inspections — a handful of bad results can produce an extreme ratio. Hover over any kommune to see the raw numbers before drawing conclusions.
