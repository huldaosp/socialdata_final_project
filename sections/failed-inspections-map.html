Geography of bad inspections

Where do bad inspections happen?

The map explores whether failed inspections are concentrated in specific parts of Denmark. reas shown in red have a higher share of failed inspections than the national average, while green areas have fewer. Use the category buttons to filter by business category.

The overall map does show geographic variation. Frederiksberg (2.36×), Vallensbæk (2.40×), and København (1.89×) all appear elevated, and a handful of Jutland kommuner like Odder (2.26×) also stand out. But this picture shifts considerably when you filter by category. That is the more interesting finding: much of what looks like a geographic pattern in the overall view is actually a reflection of what kinds of businesses are concentrated in those areas, not something inherently local about the places themselves.

Small kommuner should also be read with caution. Vallensbæk has only around 300 inspections, so a handful of bad results can push a ratio that high without representing a real underlying pattern. Odder, with 1,575 inspections, is a more reliable number, but without knowing the business type composition there it is hard to say whether the elevation is structural or coincidental.

The map is best used as an exploratory tool rather than a definitive ranking — hover over any kommune to see the raw numbers behind the color.
