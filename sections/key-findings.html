Key Findings

Our analysis of Danish restaurant health inspection data reveals several important patterns:

  • 🍽️ Overall Cleanliness: 78% of restaurants received satisfactory ratings
  • 🏙️ Urban vs Rural: Copenhagen restaurants show higher compliance rates
  • 🍕 By Cuisine: Italian restaurants have the highest average scores
  • 📊 Trends: Food safety standards have improved 15% over the past 2 years
