It is worth noting who these operators actually are. Most of the top twelve are hospitals, university canteens, and publicly run institutions. The kind of organisations with dedicated food safety staff, standardised procurement, and relatively little turnover. The data skews towards those with the most structural resources.

And yet size and resources are no guarantee. On June 2, 2025, the Danish Veterinary and Food Administration inspected all eleven KFC restaurants in Denmark on the same day and not a single one received a happy smiley. Four got an angry smiley for serious or repeated violations. Seven got a straight face. Inspectors found mould-like growth, cobwebs, and chicken stored at 13–15 °C in rooms at 24 °C. At one location, employees were photographing expired lettuce and asking management whether to throw it out, management told them to wait until the next day.
