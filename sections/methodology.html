Data & methods

How we did this analysis

All data comes from Fødevarestyrelsen — the Danish Veterinary and Food Administration — which publishes inspection results publicly through the Smiley scheme.

Dataset
165,478 inspection records across 51,715 unique food businesses. Each business can have up to 4 inspections stored — we used the full history for trend analysis and the most recent inspection for snapshot comparisons.
Time period
Inspections from January 2012 through April 2026. The public dataset stores at most four inspections per business — so for long-running businesses, only the four most recent visits are available.
Risk calculation
Relative risk is the share of inspections with score 3 or 4 for a given group, divided by the overall average. A value of 1.0× equals the average. Values above 1.0× indicate higher-than-average risk of a serious outcome.

The inspection score system

Score 1 — No remarks
Score 2 — Minor remarks / Instruction
Score 3 — Injunction (must fix)
Score 4 — Serious / Shutdown risk
