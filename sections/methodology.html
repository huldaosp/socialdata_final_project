Data & methods

How we did this analysis

All data originates from Fødevarestyrelsen — the Danish Veterinary and Food Administration — which publishes food inspection results publicly through the Danish Smiley scheme.

165,478 inspection records
51,715 unique businesses
2012–2025 years covered
The dataset

The dataset covers food establishments across Denmark — restaurants, cafés, bakeries, supermarkets, canteens, producers, and any other business handling food products.

Each record includes:

  • Business category
  • Inspection date & location
  • Inspection outcome (score 1–4)

Each establishment stores up to four of its most recent inspections.

Scope & processing

Even though the oldest records date back to 2003, reliable digital Smiley reports were only consistently available across Denmark from 2012 onwards. Older records were excluded to avoid inconsistencies from the early rollout years.

The dataset originally included around 50 business categories. To make the analysis easier to understand and compare, these were grouped into 7 broader business types used throughout the analysis.

The Danish Smiley inspection scores — hover to learn more

Score 1 — No remarks
No violations detected. The business meets all food safety requirements.
Score 2 — Minor issues
Small violations found that need correction, but no immediate risk to food safety.
Score 3 — Injunction
Formal corrective orders issued by authorities. The business must act to resolve violations.
Score 4 — Serious violations
Severe food safety concerns or critical non-compliance. May result in closure.
