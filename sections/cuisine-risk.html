Cuisine type — within restaurants

Does the type of cuisine matter too?

Restaurants make up more than 20,000 locations in the dataset, making them the largest business category by far.
The chart below explores whether inspection outcomes vary across different cuisine types using each restaurant's most recent inspection result.

⚠
Cuisine types are estimated from restaurant names
The inspection dataset does not include an official cuisine category, so cuisines were identified using keywords in restaurant names. Only 22.9% of restaurants (roughly 1 in 4) could be matched to a cuisine group this way — restaurants with descriptive names are over-represented.
These results should therefore be interpreted as exploratory patterns rather than exact rankings.
Each restaurant is counted exactly once using its most recent inspection only. Relative risk = (share of that cuisine group with Score ≥ 2) ÷ (2.65% restaurant-wide average). Only cuisines with ≥ 30 keyword-matched restaurants are shown. Cuisines identified by keyword in business name — see methodology note above.
↗
Highest observed risk
Italian and Indian restaurants show ≥3× the average risk of any inspection issue — though note the caveat on name-based classification above.
△
Elevated but consistent
Kebab / Shawarma, Burger, Pizza, Thai cluster around 1.7–1.8× the average — elevated but not dramatically so. Café/Coffee sits just above average at 1.12×.
↘
Lowest observed risk
Sushi / Japanese restaurants perform below the restaurant average — at 0.80× — the only cuisine group to do so in this dataset.
